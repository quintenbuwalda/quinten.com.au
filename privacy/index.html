AussieStats Privacy Policy

Last Updated: Tuesday, 5th September 2023

Definitions

  • "App" or "Application" refers to AussieStats, the iOS mobile application.
  • "We," "Us," or "Our" refers to the entity behind the development and management of the App.
  • "You" refers to the individual user of the App.
  • "ABB" refers to AussieBroadband, the service we utilise to provide you with information about your service plan.

Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the Application and explains how we manage your data. The Application operates exclusively within Australia and New Zealand.

Data Collection

We do not collect, store, or transmit any of your data to our servers. The only information stored locally on your device is a cookie retrieved from AussieBroadband.

Data Usage

The Application uses AussieBroadband's API to get information about your service plan. Your user credentials are sent directly to AussieBroadband to obtain this cookie. No other data is sent or stored.

Data Deletion

To delete all data stored by the Application, you have two options:

  • Delete the Application from your device.
  • Follow the in-app instructions to logout.

Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, you may contact us at https://quinten.com.au/support/.

Changes to This Policy

We reserve the right to update or change this Privacy Policy at any time. The date of the most recent update will be displayed at the top of this page.
